            <div class="mb-5 mt-5">
              <h3>How Does a Two-Port Vacuum Glove Box Work?</h3>
              <p>The required samples, components or tools are placed inside the enclosed working chamber. The chamber is then sealed and connected to the appropriate vacuum or gas-control system.</p>
              <p>For vacuum applications, a compatible pump removes air from the chamber until the required operating condition is achieved. For controlled-atmosphere applications, the chamber may be purged with nitrogen, argon or another suitable process gas. The operator then handles the internal materials through the attached gloves while observing the process through the transparent viewing panel.</p>
              <p>The achievable vacuum level and atmospheric purity depend on the chamber design, sealing system, pump selection, gas arrangement and process requirements.</p>
