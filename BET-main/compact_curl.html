Controlled Laboratory Environments for Academic & Industrial Research

In university research and academic laboratories, space efficiency, affordability, and containment performance are critical. The Compact University Glove Box, designed and manufactured by Burhani Engineering Technology, is a versatile containment system that provides a highly stable, controlled draft-free environment for handling moisture-sensitive materials, chemical compounds, and advanced electronics.

Built using optically clear materials, this University Glove Box delivers excellent visibility and ergonomic operator handling. It serves as an indispensable tool for multiple disciplines, including laboratory glove box research in nanotechnology, biotechnology, and material sciences. By using integrated purging valves, oxygen and humidity are easily purged using inert gases like Nitrogen or Argon, ensuring a pristine environment for active metal manipulation and battery research.

As a global laboratory equipment manufacturer, we integrate essential safety and operational utilities into this system. Every unit features a built-in side transfer chamber that allows instruments and materials to be imported without disrupting the internal atmosphere. With models ranging from two-port research configurations to larger four-port options (BET810-SG10, SG20, SG30, and SG40), our systems offer universities and industrial R&D facilities the gold standard of airtight isolation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers to common queries regarding academic laboratory containment systems.

A Compact University Glove Box is an airtight containment system designed for academic and laboratory research. Built using optically clear materials, it features integrated purging valves, a side transfer chamber, and safety filtration to provide a controlled draft-free atmosphere for moisture-sensitive and reactive materials.

The primary difference lies in their capacity and dimensions. The BET810-SG10 is space-efficient with internal dimensions of 28"w x 23"d x 29"h, whereas the BET810-SG20 is a medium-capacity system measuring 35"w x 29"d x 30"h, providing a larger workspace.

All models in the SG range are suitable depending on the equipment size. The BET810-SG30 is a popular choice for battery research because it features three glove ports, allowing improved operator accessibility for assembling coin cells, pouch cells, and handling reactive lithium materials.

The side transfer chamber (antechamber) is a 12-inch cylindrical chamber equipped with purging valves and a vacuum gauge. It allows the safe transfer of tools, samples, and materials in and out of the main compartment without exposing the internal inert atmosphere to ambient air.

The glove box pressure relief valve is fitted with a compact HEPA filter canister. During pressure adjustments, gas passing through the relief valve is filtered, trapping airborne particulates and preventing contaminants from entering or escaping the enclosure.
